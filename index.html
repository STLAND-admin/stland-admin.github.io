Yinji ShenTu

I am a Ph.D. student in the ZJU3DV group at Zhejiang University, where I have studied since 2024 under the supervision of Xiaowei Zhou and Wei Hua. I previously worked as a research assistant in the group. I received a B.S. from Dalian University of Technology and an M.Sc. from The University of Hong Kong. I also worked as an algorithm research intern at Hikvision on improving CCTV image quality.

My research spans computer vision and graphics, with a focus on learning-based reconstruction and editing of volumetric video, including video reconstructed from monocular footage.
